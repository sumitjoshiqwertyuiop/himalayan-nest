Plan your journey to Dwarahat

How to reach

Dwarahat is a hill town in Almora district, reached mainly by road from Ranikhet, Almora and Kumaon’s plains gateways.

Nearest railway station

Kathgodam (Haldwani) is the commonly used railhead for Kumaon. Distance to be confirmed

Nearest airport

Pantnagar Airport is the usual airport for Kumaon, with onward travel by road. Distance to be confirmed

Road connectivity

State and district roads link Dwarahat with Ranikhet and Almora. Hill roads are winding; allow extra time.

Best time to visit

Spring (March–May) and autumn (October–November) are generally pleasant. Monsoon can bring landslides; winters are cold. General guidance

Local transport

Shared taxis and local buses operate in the region; private cabs are usually arranged in advance. Confirm schedules locally
